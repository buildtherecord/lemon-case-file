their own secure channel. But fill this in privately BEFORE any offer arrives, so
you can evaluate an offer in minutes instead of under pressure.

## What they may deduct

Most statutes allow a mileage/use offset, commonly computed from miles driven
before the FIRST report of the defect. Know your jurisdiction's formula and
compute it yourself in advance:

- Miles at first defect report: ______ (from the earliest RO or report)
- Statutory formula result: ______

## Evaluating an offer

Ask for the calculation in writing, line by line. Check: is the use offset
computed from first-report mileage (not current)? Are fees, taxes, and finance
charges included? Are your documented incidentals included? Does the payoff go to
the lender directly, and what happens if payoff differs by the payoff date? Is
any confidentiality/non-disparagement clause attached, and what exactly does it
cover? Have someone qualified read anything you're asked to sign.
