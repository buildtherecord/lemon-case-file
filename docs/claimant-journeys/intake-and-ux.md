# Intake wizard and claimant UX — what the two-layer model changes

Status: draft v0. Not legal advice. Builds on `safety-gates.md`, `decision-check.md` and `two-layer-model.md`.

Each feature line below carries one status word. **built**: code in this repo does it today. **partial**: some of it does, and the line says what. **planned**: a design only; nothing builds it yet. Lines about the wizard are all **planned** because the wizard is not in this repository.

## The model
- **Outward page:** what anyone you send it to may read: the other side, a reviewer, a mediator, family, the media. Generated by `tools/views/build_home.py`, stamped with "Last updated" and a snapshot id, only the items the claimant released. **Partial:** the builder shows an item unless it is switched off (`outward: false` on a fact, `share: false` on a document); opt-in release is planned.
- **Inward record:** for the claimant and the claimant's attorney. The full linked views generated by `tools/views/build_views.py` (**built**), plus counsel-only material once an attorney is involved and directs the work (**planned**: how it is stored so claimant tooling cannot read or leak it is an open question below). Maintained deliberately: nothing is deleted.
- **There is no layer in between.** Anything family should see goes on the outward page.

Privilege protects attorney-client communications and attorney work product, not facts or documents that already exist. A record built before hiring a lawyer is generally not privileged, and "held back" is not "privileged." The UI must never imply otherwise. A counsel-only space exists only once an attorney directs the work.

## What changes in the intake wizard
1. **The first screen is a 30-second capture** (photo, voice, text, forward an email). Nothing is shared at that point, so nothing needs asking. Sorting into facts and exhibits comes after. *Planned.*
2. **Right after the first capture, and before anything can be shared or any date or path screen appears, ask two things.** Who will see this: "Just me / my attorney / anyone else (family, the other side, a reviewer, the media)." "Anyone else" means the outward page, and nothing goes there until chosen. Then the state, and "any lawyer involved?" (required, as in the safety gates). The answers set defaults (private by default) and can change later. *Planned.*
3. **"Any lawyer involved?" also decides counsel-only material:** that space appears only after the claimant says an attorney is involved, with the attorney's name and a note that the attorney decides what goes in it. *Planned.*
4. **Every fact gets a status at entry:** shows in a document / my account / disputed. "My account" is a normal answer, not a failure. The wizard prompts "what document might show this?" but never blocks. *Planned* in the wizard; the record's `status` field and its three labels are **built** in both builders.
5. **Sharing is a per-item decision with two switches:** the fact (`outward`) and its document (`share`). Documents with other people's information, health data, financial identifiers, or full message exports should default to *not shared*, with a masking step offered. *Partial:* both switches are **built** in `build_home.py`, which shows an item unless it is switched off; default-not-shared and the masking step are planned.
6. **Deadlines are entered by the claimant with a source** ("where is this written?" and "who told you?"). No computed deadlines; the empty state says "none entered does not mean none applies." *Partial:* `build_views.py` refuses a deadline with no source or one marked computed, and the brief's empty state says so (**built**); the wizard prompts are planned.
7. **Offers and contacts from the other side get their own quick-add** ("they called," "they sent a release"), with the "don't sign yet" micro-journey. *Planned.* The brief has fields for offers and for what was sent to the other side (**built**).
8. **Intervals and counts are never typed.** The wizard asks for dates; the page computes days and totals. *Partial:* both builders compute counts and intervals from two fact ids, and the outward page computes its "Day N" tile (**built**); the wizard is planned, and no check yet flags a number typed into prose.
9. **Plain tone, pause-able.** Every step can be saved and resumed; no deadline reveals late at night; 988 and peer-support links reachable from any screen. *Planned.*

## What changes in the rest of the UX
- **Share gate.** Before any outward snapshot is created: who is receiving it, whether settlement or confidentiality terms exist, whether it names people or businesses beyond what the documents say, and "has an attorney read this?" Defamation and confidentiality reminders appear here, not buried in help. *Planned.*
- **Sent copies.** Each outward send saves a dated, hashed snapshot so the claimant can show exactly what left their hands. The live record keeps changing; the snapshot does not. *Partial:* `build_home.py` computes the snapshot id from the page content and writes `snapshot.json`; it clears the output folder on each build, and keeping each sent copy with its recipient is planned.
- **Review states on an outward page:** Draft → Read by me → Read by my attorney (optional, recommended) → Sent. A page cannot be marked Sent without the share gate. *Planned.*
- **Share report.** A private report lists what was excluded, which facts lack a shareable document, and what the page says about that. It never appears on the page. *Built* (`share-report.txt`); today it is written into the same folder as the page, so keep it out of anything you publish.
- **"Last updated" at the top of every page, on both layers** (**built**). **Snapshot id** at the top of the outward page (**built**); the inward record is live and has none.
- **Gaps page for the claimant.** Facts with no document, documents no fact cites, topics with nothing documented, deadlines nobody has confirmed. Framed as "what a lawyer will ask," not as failure. *Built* (`gaps.html`).
- **Inward brief.** The inward record's brief page is the attorney's first hour: intake fields, claimant-entered deadlines with their sources, cast, what has been sent to the other side, prior lawyer, gaps. *Built* (`index.html` from `build_views.py`).
- **One outward page for every outside reader.** There is no separate media view. Whether the media needs a stricter edition than the other side is an open question. Any send goes through the share gate. *Planned* (the gate).
- **Exit paths.** "Close and archive" and "pause" as visible as "send." Archive keeps the record, the snapshots, and the share report; closing never deletes anything. *Planned.*

## Open questions
- Which wording needs attorney review per state before launch (see `safety-gates.md`).
- How counsel-only material is stored so an attorney controls it and the claimant tooling cannot read or leak it.
- Whether the wizard should offer to mask documents itself or hand off to a documented masking procedure (a procedure used on a real case did OCR-verified burn-in; it is repeatable but not yet a user feature).
- Whether the media needs its own stricter outward edition, separate from the one the other side reads.
- The current wizard is not in the repository; these notes apply to whatever form it takes and should be checked against it.


## Decisions come with their evidence (product rule)
Any screen that asks a claimant, a helper or counsel to decide something shows the evidence beside the question: the document image or the exact quote, the current text and the proposed text side by side with the reason, and the effect of each answer. Answers are saved with who answered and when, so a build can read them. A decision with no evidence on screen is not asked.

## Anonymity switches
Names are personal information with a switch each: individuals' names (anonymous or open) and company names (open or anonymous), with law firms treated as their own kind. When individuals are anonymous, each person keeps one role label everywhere, shown in square brackets, including inside quotations, so a reader can see an editorial replacement was made. Roles come from a document or from the claimant; the tool does not guess them. Images get the same covers from the same switch, and an image that cannot be covered with confidence stays off the outward page. The quote checker accepts a bracketed role label in place of a name and nothing else.

## Attorney-review tags
A fixed tag, "Counsel to confirm", with one line saying what to confirm, can sit on a fact, a sentence, a document, a rule or a page. States: open, confirmed by counsel (date and initials), declined (with reason). Inward pages list their open tags first; the outward page never shows the tags but will not build while one in its content is open.
